# UNTRAPD Community Code of Conduct

## Our Values

**UNTRAPD is built on**:
- Authenticity over perfection
- Community over competition
- Vulnerability over performance
- Collaboration over individualism

## Expected Behavior

✅ **Be Real**: Share failures, not just wins. Messy middle is valuable.
✅ **Be Supportive**: Help without keeping score. Give freely, receive graciously.
✅ **Be Respectful**: Diverse paths to freedom are valid. No gatekeeping.
✅ **Be Transparent**: Build in public. Share learnings, even mistakes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

❌ Performative positivity (toxic positivity)
❌ Gatekeeping or elitism
❌ Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
❌ Spam, self-promotion without value
❌ Plagiarism or stealing others' work

## Enforcement

Violations will result in:
1. Warning and guidance
2. Temporary ban from community spaces
3. Permanent ban for severe/repeated violations
